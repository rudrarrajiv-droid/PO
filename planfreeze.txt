1. jab bhi job card banate samay item name aur quantity daalu to job card to ban jaaye but uska print and pdf nahi generate hona chahiye
2. print and pdf ke liye job card ka issue hona jaruri hai 
3. job card tabhi issue hoga jab reel allocate hogi
4. job card banate samay jab hum quantity aur item name dalege to usme bhi reel allocation ka option aana chahiye agar hame job card banate samay hi reel allocate karni hai to bhi hum usi samay allocate kar sake aur agar job card banate samay reel allocate ho jaati hai to issue ka option bhi usi me aa jana chahiye isme toda time bachega pocess wahi hai ya to continuously chalte jao aut time bacha lo ya phir skip karke job card submit to ho jayega but bina reel allocation ke issue nahi hoga aur bina issue ke job card print and pdf nahi Banega
5. kisi bhi jaldbazi me ya urgency me sirf admin ya Packwell se login karne par hi reel allocation skip karke issue kar sakte hai aur print and pdf nikal sakte hai but user login aisa nahi kar sakta
6. ha sabse jaruri baat reel jaise hi allocate hogi waise hi wo reel jitna weight reel ka allocate hoga utna weight us reel ka freeze ho jaana chahiye dusri layer ya dusri job card ki allocation me wo reel ka allocate weight show nahi hona chahiye
7. jaise example reel ka weight 650 kg hai aur reel allocate hui hai 200kg to bachi hui reel weight 450 kg hi dikhayega usi job ki next paper layer me aur next job card reel allocation me wo reel 450kg hi show kare but freeze reels ka sign kuch aisa de do jisse freeze reels ko dekhkar hi pata lag jaaye ki ye reel kisi job card ke chalte freeze ya use me hai used weight ke saath 
8. job finder aur reverse calculator and job card in teeno ke case me reels ka freeze weight ko use nahi karega reel freeze weight ke alawa bacha hua weight hi show karega
9. reel inventory me reel history me search reel no ke saath paper type ka option bhi matching ke saath de do jaise paper type me sk ya vk ya deuplex dale to uske anushar result bhi aaye
10. kisi reel ka kuch bhi weight agar kahi use ya freeze hai to us reel ko dekhte hi pata lag jaaye aisa kuch sign banao
11. in sab baato ka reel inventory me kuch effect nahi hona chahiye reel inventory actual hi rahni chahiye jab tak ki usme manual in ya out na ho
