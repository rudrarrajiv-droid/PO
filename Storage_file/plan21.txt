STEP 5 — MONTHLY MOVEMENT
============================================================

The monthly view must clearly distinguish:

PO MASTER INFORMATION

from

MONTHLY MOVEMENT.

For the selected month, show the relevant:

IN QTY

OUT QTY

Do NOT confuse monthly IN/OUT with the PO's lifetime total IN/OUT.

Example:

PO lifetime IN = 1000

July IN = 300

August IN = 700

When July is selected:

Monthly IN = 300

Lifetime IN remains = 1000

Do NOT overwrite lifetime totals.

============================================================
STEP 6 — OPENING POSITION FOR MONTH
============================================================

Where required for monthly movement, calculate the month's opening
position from existing transaction history.

Conceptually:

MONTH OPENING BALANCE

=

PO quantity position immediately before the selected month.

Do NOT create a separate stored monthly balance.

Calculate it from existing PO and transaction data.

Example:

Opening PO = 1000

June OUT = 200

July OUT = 300

For July:

Opening Balance = 800

July OUT = 300

July Closing Position = 500

Do not modify the underlying transactions.

============================================================
STEP 7 — MONTHLY IN
============================================================

For the selected month:

MONTHLY IN QTY

must equal the sum of IN transactions whose transaction date falls
inside that selected month.

Do not include transactions from other months.

============================================================
STEP 8 — MONTHLY OUT
============================================================

For the selected month:

MONTHLY OUT QTY

must equal the sum of OUT transactions whose transaction date falls
inside that selected month.

Do not include transactions from other months.

============================================================
STEP 9 — MONTHLY CLOSING POSITION
============================================================

Calculate the monthly closing position from the opening position and
that month's movement.

Conceptually:

MONTHLY CLOSING
=
MONTH OPENING
+ MONTHLY IN
- MONTHLY OUT

Do NOT overwrite the PO's current Closing Balance.

The monthly view is a reporting view only.
